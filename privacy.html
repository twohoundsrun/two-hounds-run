Privacy Policy

Effective September 5, 2026 · Covers ArenaTap on Reddit and other Two Hounds Run projects linked from this page.

Two Hounds Run (“we”, “us”) builds small sports and utility apps. This page explains what ArenaTap collects when you use the Reddit app.

Who this applies to

People who open ArenaTap inside a Reddit post or community where a moderator has installed the app.

What we collect

  • Your Reddit username and Reddit user id, provided by Reddit when you are signed in.
  • The community name and post context Reddit sends with the session.
  • That day’s guesses, locked answers, score, and completion status.
  • A public leaderboard row for completed games in that community (username and score).

If you are signed out, you can view the game but we do not save a ranked score to your account.

What we do not collect

  • Email, phone number, payment details, or precise device location.
  • A private profile beyond the Reddit identity Reddit already exposes to installed apps.
  • Analytics pixels or advertising identifiers of our own.

Where it is stored

Progress and leaderboard data are stored in Reddit’s Redis for the installed app, keyed to that community and UTC day. We do not run a separate ArenaTap user database.

Map imagery

The playable map currently uses a bundled Natural Earth atlas. If Reddit later approves server.arcgisonline.com, the app may request public map tiles from Esri. Those requests contain tile coordinates, not your Reddit username.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. Scores appear on the in-community board and in any spoiler-free result text you copy yourself. Reddit also processes the session under Reddit’s own privacy policy.

Retention

Daily challenge records remain until the app is removed from the community or we add a retention cleanup. Uninstalling the app from a community removes our stored Redis data for that install.

Children

ArenaTap is a sports geography game intended for Reddit users. It is not directed at children under 13.
